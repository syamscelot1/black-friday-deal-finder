How we check a deal

  1. Read, never guess. A price only makes this page if a deals source showed it — naming the store, the sale price and the usual price — when we checked on 27 Nov 2026. Deals with conflicting prices across sources were left out.
  2. Discounts are computed, not quoted. The % off on every card is (usual − sale) ÷ usual from the two prices shown. If the math does not add up, the deal does not belong here.
  3. Store links go to the store. The product page when the research exposed one; otherwise the store's own search for that exact product. No product URL was ever invented.
  4. The verdict is honest. “Record low” only when a tracker called it an all-time or record low. 40%+ off list is a strong deal; 20–39% a good deal; under 20% a modest deal — even when it is the only console deal of the year.

Prices are in US dollars, before tax, as checked on 27 Nov 2026 (WITA). Black Friday prices move by the hour — the store page is the final word. Black Friday Vault is an independent deal finder and is not affiliated with any store named here.
